# Readiness Register — Configd Production-Readiness Pipeline (Sessions 1–7)

**Purpose.** This is the single source of truth for the 7-session production-readiness pipeline.
Every finding from the Session-1 ground-truth audit (`docs/audit-session-1/*`) and every still-open
risk carried in from the pre-pipeline ledger lives here as one row. **This register supersedes
`docs/READINESS-LEDGER.md` §3 as the live risk register going forward**; the old ledger remains in
place as historical record and is not extended.

**Severity definitions (charter):**
- **P0** — safety violation, data-loss risk, system cannot run, or a core-guarantee claim is FICTION/CONTRADICTED.
- **P1** — correctness risk, major guarantee untested, hot-path constraint violated.
- **P2** — quality / operability gap.
- **P3** — polish.

**Status values:** `OPEN` / `IN-PROGRESS` / `RESOLVED` / `ACCEPTED-RISK`.
`ACCEPTED-RISK` requires a written justification in the row and is mandatorily re-reviewed in
Session 7. A row is `RESOLVED` only with concrete resolution evidence (command + output or
file:line) in the last column.

**Ownership rule:** every finding has an owner session in 2–7. Session names: **2** Correctness
(simulation, linearizability, property tests, jcstress, formal) · **3** Durability, Recovery &
Chaos · **4** Performance & Capacity · **5** Security & Supply Chain · **6** Operability &
Deployment · **7** Adversarial Release Review. Where a finding plausibly fit two sessions, the
earlier owns it and the row notes re-validation.

Severity authority: `docs/audit-session-1/triage-reproductions.md` is final for all P0/P1 rows
(two-agent reproduction rule); its recorded disputes are preserved in the rows below.
